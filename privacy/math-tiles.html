Privacy Policy — Math Tiles

Last updated: September 1, 2026

This policy applies to Math Tiles ("the App"), a number puzzle game developed by Yunjin ("the Developer").

1. What we collect

Nothing. The App collects no personal information of any kind. There are no user accounts, no sign-in, no servers, no advertising, and no analytics.

2. No network access

The released App is built without the Android internet permission. It cannot send or receive data over a network, even if it wanted to. You can verify this yourself in the app's permission list.

3. What is stored on your device

The App saves the following on your device only:

  • Your current puzzle in progress
  • Which stages you have cleared, and your stars and best scores
  • Your best score in Endless mode
  • Your settings (theme, vibration)

Uninstalling the App deletes all of it.

4. Android backup

Android may copy this saved data to your own Google Drive through the system's Auto Backup feature, so your progress returns when you reinstall the App or set up a new device.

This backup belongs to you, not to the Developer. The Developer has no access to it. You can turn it off in Settings → Google → Backup.

5. Children

The App is safe for all ages. It collects nothing, shows no advertising, and contains no links out to other services.

6. Changes

If the App ever adds a feature that collects data, this page will be updated before that version is released.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy: yunjinlvlv@gmail.com
