Privacy Policy — Math Tiles

Last updated: September 1, 2026

This policy applies to Math Tiles ("the App"), a number puzzle game developed by Yunjin ("the Developer").

1. What we collect

Nothing at all, unless you join the leaderboard. The leaderboard is optional. Until you choose a name for it, the App sends nothing anywhere.

Once you have chosen a name, the following is sent when an Endless run ends:

  • The display name you typed (up to 12 characters)
  • Your score
  • The level you reached
  • An anonymous identifier

The anonymous identifier is a random string generated by the App. It is not linked to your name, email, phone number, Google account or device ID. Its only purpose is to recognise your own entry so a new score replaces your old one instead of adding a duplicate.

Your display name is shown publicly to every other player. Do not use your real name or any contact detail.

2. What we do not collect

No accounts and no sign-in. No advertising. No analytics or tracking. No location, contacts, photos, or device identifiers. Nothing about how you play is recorded.

The entire game — every stage and Endless mode — works offline. The leaderboard is the only part that needs a network.

3. Network access

The App requests the Android internet permission for the leaderboard and for nothing else.

When there is no connection, the App shows the last leaderboard it received and the game continues to work normally.

4. What is stored on your device

The App saves the following on your device only:

  • Your current puzzle in progress
  • Which stages you have cleared, and your stars and best scores
  • Your best score in Endless mode
  • Your settings (theme, vibration)
  • Your leaderboard name
  • A copy of the last leaderboard received, so it can be shown offline

Uninstalling the App deletes all of it.

5. Android backup

Android may copy this saved data to your own Google Drive through the system's Auto Backup feature, so your progress returns when you reinstall the App or set up a new device.

This backup belongs to you, not to the Developer. The Developer has no access to it. You can turn it off in Settings → Google → Backup.

6. Where leaderboard data is held

Leaderboard entries are stored in Google Firebase Realtime Database, on servers located in Singapore. Anonymous sign-in is provided by Firebase Authentication.

Google processes this data on the Developer's behalf. See the Google privacy policy.

All transmission is encrypted in transit (TLS).

7. Keeping and deleting data

A leaderboard entry is kept until it is deleted.

To have your entry removed, email yunjinlvlv@gmail.com with the display name you used. It will be deleted.

Uninstalling the App does not delete an entry you have already submitted. It removes everything on your device, but the entry on the leaderboard remains until you ask for it to be deleted.

8. Children

The App shows no advertising and contains no links out to other services.

The leaderboard is the one place where something you type becomes visible to others. Children should not enter a real name or any contact detail there. Nothing else is collected.

9. Changes

If the App ever collects something not described here, this page and the App's Data safety entry on Google Play will be updated before that version is released.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy, or a deletion request: yunjinlvlv@gmail.com

アカウントもログインもありません。広告はありません。解析ツールや追跡もありません。位置情報・連絡先・写真・端末識別子を収集しません。どのようにプレイしたかも記録しません。

本アプリは以下を端末内にのみ保存します。

Política de privacidad — Math Tiles

Última actualización: 1 de septiembre de 2026

Esta política se aplica a Math Tiles («la App»), un juego de puzles numéricos desarrollado por Yunjin («el Desarrollador»).

1. Qué recopilamos

Nada, salvo que uses la clasificación. La clasificación es opcional. Hasta que elijas un nombre para ella, la App no envía nada a ninguna parte.

Una vez elegido el nombre, al terminar una partida del modo Infinito se envía lo siguiente:

  • El nombre que has escrito (máximo 12 caracteres)
  • Tu puntuación
  • El nivel alcanzado
  • Un identificador anónimo

El identificador anónimo es una cadena aleatoria generada por la App. No está vinculado a tu nombre, correo, teléfono, cuenta de Google ni identificador de dispositivo. Solo sirve para reconocer tu propia entrada, de modo que una puntuación nueva sustituya a la anterior en lugar de duplicarla.
