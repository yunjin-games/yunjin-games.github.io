Privacy Policy — Chain Sokoban

Last updated: September 3, 2026

This privacy policy applies to the Chain Sokoban app ("App"), a turn-based grid puzzle game developed by Yunjin Games ("Developer"). By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

The App itself does not collect any personal information. It has no user accounts, no sign-in, and no servers of its own. Data is collected only through the third-party services listed below.

The App does not collect your name, email address, phone number, or any other personally identifiable information.

2. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following services, which may collect data as described in their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob — Advertising. May collect an advertising ID, device information, approximate location, and ad interaction data.
  • Google User Messaging Platform — Consent management where required (EEA, UK and similar regions).
  • Google Play Billing — In-app purchases. Google Play processes payments; the Developer never receives your card or payment method details.

3. Advertising

A rewarded ad plays only when you choose to watch one to refill your undos. The App never forces an ad on you and shows no banner or interstitial ads. Where consent is required, a consent form appears before any ad, and you can change your choice at any time from Settings → Ad privacy options. You can also opt out of personalized ads in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization).

4. In-App Purchases

The App offers one optional one-time purchase, "Unlimited undo". Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play. The Developer stores only whether the item is owned, on your device.

5. Data Storage

Your progress (cleared stages, best move counts), settings (sound, vibration, board style, language) and tutorial state are stored locally on your device only. They are not transmitted to any external server. You can delete this data from Settings → Reset progress, or by uninstalling the App.

6. Permissions

The App requests only internet access and network state, used for ads and purchases. With no ads watched and nothing purchased, every stage is playable offline. The App does not request camera, contacts, location, or storage permissions.

7. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. It contains only gameplay, advertising and an optional purchase.

8. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

9. Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, contact the Developer at ferg7979biz@gmail.com.

Aplikasi menawarkan satu pembelian sekali bayar yang bersifat opsional, "Undo tanpa batas". Pembelian sepenuhnya ditangani Google Play; Pengembang hanya menyimpan status kepemilikan di perangkat Anda.

Progres Anda (level yang selesai, langkah terbaik), setelan (suara, getaran, gaya papan, bahasa), dan status tutorial disimpan hanya di perangkat Anda dan tidak dikirim ke server mana pun. Anda dapat menghapusnya melalui Setelan → "Atur ulang progres" atau dengan menghapus Aplikasi.

6. Izin

Aplikasi hanya meminta akses internet dan status jaringan, yang dipakai untuk iklan dan pembelian. Tanpa menonton iklan dan tanpa membeli apa pun, semua level dapat dimainkan offline. Aplikasi tidak meminta izin kamera, kontak, lokasi, atau penyimpanan.

7. Privasi anak

Aplikasi tidak dengan sengaja mengumpulkan informasi pribadi dari anak di bawah 13 tahun. Aplikasi hanya berisi permainan, iklan, dan satu pembelian opsional.
